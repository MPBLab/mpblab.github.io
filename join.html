Who we are looking for

  • Undergraduate students looking for a final-year project or thesis
  • MSc and MPhil students in mathematics, physics or a related subject
  • PhD applicants with a background in differential equations or mathematical modelling

A good grounding in ordinary and partial differential equations helps. Experience with programming (for example Python, MATLAB or Mathematica) is useful but not required.

How to apply

  1. Read about our research and pick the topics that interest you.
  2. Prepare a short CV, your academic transcript, and a paragraph on what you would like to work on.
  3. Send them to {{ site.leader }}{% if site.email != "" %} at {{ site.email }}{% else %} at the {{ site.department }}, {{ site.institution_short }}{% endif %}, with “Joining MPB Lab” in the subject line.
