## What we do

Many systems in nature are described by nonlinear differential equations:
a wave travelling through a plasma, a shock front forming in a fluid, a
population of predators and prey rising and falling together. Our lab derives
these equations from physical and biological models, solves them with
analytical and numerical methods, and asks what the solutions tell us about
the real system.

Our work falls into three connected areas:

- [Nonlinear waves in plasmas]({{ '/research/plasma-waves/' | relative_url }})
- [Nonlinear evolution equations]({{ '/research/evolution-equations/' | relative_url }})
- [Mathematical biology]({{ '/research/mathematical-biology/' | relative_url }})

## Our aims

- Produce rigorous, well-tested mathematical results that other researchers can build on.
- Train students in modelling, analysis and scientific computing.
- Collaborate with physicists, biologists and mathematicians in Bangladesh and abroad.
