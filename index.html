Abstract

We study nonlinear waves in plasmas and mathematical models of living systems. Our work derives the equations that govern ion-acoustic and positron-acoustic waves, finds their solitary-wave and shock solutions, and applies the same mathematical tools to the dynamics of populations and disease.
